Silver Price in Dubai & UAE

999 Fine Silver

99.9% pure silver, used for bars and coins. It is the benchmark for today's silver rate in UAE and is too soft for most everyday jewellery.

02

925 Sterling Silver

92.5% silver blended with copper for strength. It is the standard for fine silver jewellery in Dubai, and every SH Silver piece is 925 sterling silver.

How the rate is set

The live silver price follows the international spot market in US dollars per ounce, converted to AED at the fixed 3.6725 rate and to grams. It is the same across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah.

Questions

Silver Rate FAQs

What is the silver rate today in UAE?

The live silver rate per gram for 999 fine silver and 925 sterling silver is shown at the top of this page in AED and refreshes every minute.

What is the difference between 999 and 925 silver?

999 fine silver is 99.9% pure and very soft. 925 sterling silver is 92.5% silver mixed with copper, which makes it durable enough for rings, bracelets and everyday jewellery.

Is the silver price the same in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah?

Yes. Silver rates follow the same international price across all emirates, and the AED is pegged to the US dollar. Jewellery prices differ by design and craftsmanship.

How much is one tola of silver?

One tola equals 11.664 grams. Choose "Tola" in the calculator above to see today's silver price per tola.

Why does a silver ring cost more than its silver value?

Jewellery prices include design, craftsmanship, finishing such as rhodium plating, stones and 5% VAT, on top of the silver metal value.

How often does the silver price change?

Silver prices move throughout the day while international markets are open, Monday to Friday. On weekends the rate stays close to Friday's closing price.

Rates are indicative, based on the live international silver market, and may differ from in-store prices.
