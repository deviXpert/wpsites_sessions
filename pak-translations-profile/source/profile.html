02 · What We Do
Our services

What We Do

01Localization (App, Software, Website)

More and more companies are becoming aware of the significance of reaching out to new markets and having a presence online. Localization plays a key role in this pursuit. Fully aware of this, Pak Translations offers top-notch localization services through a pool of carefully selected, experienced localizers who are adept in the art of adapting content to local markets, while remaining sensitive to local cultural and language norms.

02Document Translation

Be it brochures, certificates, SMS messages, affidavits, court summons, medical reports, or any other document, Pak Translations has the experts who can transfer your message into other languages, maintaining both accuracy and readability.

03Editing and Proofreading

It is crucial to have your documents, websites, apps & software, and subtitles free of errors and presented in the required format and layout. This is where our expert editors and proofreaders come in. They can ensure that the final version you get is both spotless and natural sounding.

04Audio/Video Transcription & Translation

Transcription and translation of audio and video files is a demanding art requiring insertion of accurate time-codes and faithful rendition of speech, while ensuring that the translation is understandable to the readers. A high level of concentration and an ability to comprehend speech despite background noise, overlapping, and interruptions are key to success. Our translators possess the necessary skills and can meet the client’s expectations.

05Interpreting

As it needs to be done on the spot, interpreting is a very sensitive service, requiring not only a good command of the languages involved but also the ability to quickly process speech and translate it into another language in your mind. It needs profound competence, as the margin for error is almost none. Our interpreters are there to interpret for you efficiently and in different formats: in person, over the phone, and via videoconferencing. Both consecutive and simultaneous interpretation services are available.

06Subtitling

Subtitling is even more demanding than audio/video transcription, in that it requires precise synchronization between the subtitles and the time-codes, as well as preparation of files in various formats such as SubRip & VobSub and open or closed subtitles/captions. We cater to our clients’ specific subtitling needs with efficiency and accuracy.

07Content Creation

We are blessed with a pool of creative writers and expert copywriters with years of experience in writing convincing and attractive content: blog articles, social media posts, print and digital ads, case stories, email marketing content, presentations and more. Our expert writers help you reach your targeted audience in an effective and appealing way.

08Foreign Language Material Development

We provide expert services in developing reading, writing, and listening tests for foreign/second language learners in accordance with the various proficiency levels set by the clients. Apart from testing, we are experts in collating and creating listening, writing, and reading passages to be used as practice material in language learning classes.

09Linguistics-Related Services

Be it phonetic transcription, pronunciation validation, PoS (parts of speech) or case tagging, stress placement, or assistance with building a corpus or dictionary, we are here to fulfil your needs. Our expertise has been well proven in the projects we have carried out so far, and our clients’ testimonials are proof of it. Please check with us whether we currently offer these services in the required language(s).

10Desktop Publishing

We have DTP services available for our clients. Whether it is brochures, flyers, books or any other document, we can do typesetting, formatting, layout adjustment, and designing in accordance with your specific needs.

04 · Why Choose Us
Why clients choose us

Why Choose Us

01A Variety of Language Services

Professional language needs vary from document translation to website localization to various phonetic and morphosyntactic requirements to, say, developing text-to-speech (TTS) software. Keeping this in view, Pak Translations serves as a one-stop shop for all your language needs (for details, refer to our Services). We make sure to serve with top-class service and envision becoming a leading translation company in the world.

02Excellent Customer Support

We offer end-to-end services, which means that we work with our clients from the beginning of the project to the end. We go through the project instructions in detail and ask for further details or clarifications to narrow down to the client’s specific needs. Where needed, we also request reference/supporting material and, where applicable, provide relevant and helpful suggestions. We are always there to answer any queries and concerns after delivering the project.

03Quality Control

Quality control is the backbone of a successful business. As the first step, we select our language experts only after rigorously testing their skills. Our Translator Instructions (shared with all linguists as part of onboarding) bind them to check their deliverables thoroughly before delivering to us. We regularly get the work reviewed by other linguists, and we are always there to answer any post-delivery queries and concerns – a step that helps us ensure the quality is up to the client’s expectations.

04On-Time Delivery & Competitive Prices

We make sure to deliver work to clients on time. In the case of any unforeseen potential delays (such as a linguist falling ill), we inform our clients in time and take prompt action to assign the work to another linguist in our team.

We offer reasonable prices to our clients and keep their available budget in view.
