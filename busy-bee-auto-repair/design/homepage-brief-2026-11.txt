  Q1. Where is Busy Bee Auto Repair in Calgary? We’re at 324 41 Ave NE, Calgary, AB T2E
  2N3, close to Deerfoot Trail in NE Calgary. We’re open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM.
  Call (403) 930-5888 for directions or to book.

  Q2. Do I need an appointment? No. Walk-ins are welcome Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6
  PM. An appointment is the fastest way to be seen, especially during winter tire season
  when the shop is busiest.

  Q3. Do you work on all makes and models? Yes. We service domestic, Asian and European
  cars, SUVs and light trucks. If you drive a specialty or exotic vehicle, call us first so we can
  confirm we can help.

  Q4. How much does a diagnostic cost? We quote the diagnostic upfront. We explain what
  we find and give you a repair estimate. We never start repairs without your approval.     ⚠️
  CONFIRM: if the owner gives a price, add it to this answer.

  Q5. Do your repairs come with a warranty? Most repairs include a parts and labour
  warranty. Terms vary by repair and parts used, so ask us about the coverage on your
  specific job.

  🟥 NEW Q6. How much does a Mobil 1 synthetic oil change cost? Mobil 1 synthetic oil
  changes start from $75 and include a free vehicle inspection. The final price depends on
  your vehicle’s oil capacity and filter. Call us with your make, model and year for an exact
  quote.

  🟥 NEW Q7. Is the battery test really free? Yes. We offer free battery testing and a
  charging-system check. If the battery or alternator needs replacing, we quote that
  separately and you approve it before we begin.

  🟥 NEW Q8. How do I know if my brakes need attention? Warning signs include
  squealing, grinding, vibration when braking, a soft pedal, the car pulling to one side, or
  longer stopping distances. If you notice any of these, get your brakes inspected as soon
  as possible.

  🟥 NEW Q9. Do I need winter tires in Calgary? Alberta does not require winter tires, but
  many drivers fit them for better grip in cold, snow and ice. A common guideline is to
  switch when temperatures stay near or below 7°C. We offer seasonal tire swaps,
  balancing and tire checks.

  🟥 NEW Q10. How often should I change my oil? Follow the interval in your owner’s
  manual or your vehicle’s oil-life monitor. Short trips, cold starts and towing count as severe
  driving and can call for shorter intervals. We check your oil during every oil change
  inspection.
