Oct 8, 2026 · @Ahsan Ali

## Overview

Busy Bee Auto Repair can realistically rank for "auto repair Calgary" (4,400 searches/month, difficulty 13) by rebuilding the site around one page per high-demand service, with clean on-page SEO. The current site has the services but wastes most of its ranking potential through duplicate pages, missing meta descriptions, and thin or generic copy.

**Audit findings on the current site:**

1. **Duplicate pages competing with each other:** /engine-repair/ and /engine-repair-in-calgary/ are identical (3,760 words each). /transmission/ and /transmission-repair/ also overlap. Pick one URL per service and 301-redirect the other.
2. **Missing meta descriptions:** only the engine and brake pages have one. Every other page shows Google whatever text it picks.
3. **Generic page titles:** most titles read "Battery Replacement - Busy Bee Auto Repair", with no "Calgary" and no searchable phrase.
4. **Conflicting hours:** the homepage says both "Monday to Friday 9AM to 6PM" and "Monday to Friday & Sunday, closed Saturdays". Inconsistent hours hurt local rankings and trust.
5. **Pricing copy errors:** the "Brakes & Rotors" price box says "$84 for up to 16 inch tire", copied from the tire repair box.
6. **Off-topic and broken copy:** the transmission page lists "Spark Plug Replacement" as a transmission problem, and a price reads "149 − 149−199". Several pages have typos ("Maintainance", "recieved", "serpentitension").
7. **Unused high-demand services:** tires, wheel alignment, and oil change carry the biggest low-difficulty search demand, but alignment has no page of its own.

**Strategy:** keep the homepage targeting the core "auto repair / car repair / mechanic Calgary" cluster. Give every high-volume service its own focused page with "Calgary" in the title, H1, URL, and meta description. Use NE Calgary wording to win nearby searches, and back it all with LocalBusiness (AutoRepair) schema and a consistent name, address, and phone everywhere.
