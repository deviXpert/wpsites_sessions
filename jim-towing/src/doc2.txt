	What is flatbed towing? Flatbed towing uses a truck with a flat, tilting platform. The vehicle is winched onto the bed and secured with straps, so no wheels roll on the road during transport. This is why it is the safest towing method for many vehicles.
Flatbed vs. hook-and-chain (wheel-lift) towing: which is better? Flatbed is better for AWD and 4WD vehicles, low-clearance cars, luxury vehicles, motorcycles, and vehicles with damaged wheels, axles, or transmissions. Wheel-lift towing can be fine for some front-wheel-drive vehicles over short distances, but it puts wear on the lifted wheels. If you are unsure, ask us and we will recommend the right method.
Which vehicles need a flatbed tow?
* AWD/4WD vehicles: Towing with drive wheels on the ground can damage the drivetrain.
* Luxury and lowered cars: Low front bumpers and splitters need a low-angle bed.
* Motorcycles: Secured upright with proper tie-downs.
* Accident-damaged vehicles: No further stress on damaged parts.
* Non-running or non-rolling vehicles: Loaded by winch.
How we protect your vehicle
* Professional flatbed equipment and winching
* Wheel straps and safe tie-down methods
* Careful loading angle for low vehicles
* Inspection of loading points before we move your car
