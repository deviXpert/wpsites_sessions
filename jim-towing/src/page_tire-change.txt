### h2 Tire change FAQs
Common questions about roadside tire help.
### h3 Do I need a spare tire?
A usable spare is best. If you don’t have one (or it’s flat), we can tow you to a tire shop.
### h3 Can you change a tire on the highway?
Safety comes first. If the location isn’t safe, we may recommend towing to a safer area.
### h3 Will you repair the tire?
We don’t patch tires roadside, but we can help you get to a shop for repair or replacement.
### h3 How long does it take?
It depends on your location and conditions. Call for an ETA and we’ll dispatch quickly.
