Frequently Asked Questions

How is today's gold rate in UAE calculated?

The rate follows the live international gold spot price (USD per troy ounce), converted to AED at the fixed rate of 3.6725 and to grams (1 troy ounce = 31.1035 g). Lower karats are priced by their gold purity.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K, 21K and 18K gold?

24K is pure gold (99.9%). 22K has 91.6% gold, 21K has 87.5% and 18K has 75%. Lower karats contain more alloy metals, which makes them stronger and better suited to everyday jewellery.

How much is one tola of gold?

One tola equals 11.664 grams. Select "Tola" above to see today's price per tola for each karat.

Is the jewellery price the same as the gold rate?

No. Jewellery prices include the gold value, making charges for the design and craftsmanship, and 5% VAT. Use the calculator above for an estimate.

Is the gold rate the same in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah?

Yes. Gold rates are broadly the same across all emirates because they follow the same international gold price and the AED is pegged to the US dollar. Small differences come from each jeweller's making charges.

How often does the gold price in UAE change?

Gold prices move all day while international markets are open, Monday to Friday. This page refreshes the live rate every minute. On weekends the rate stays close to Friday's closing price.
