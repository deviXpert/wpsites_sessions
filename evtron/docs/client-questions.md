# EVTRON website: questions for the client

Please answer what you can. Anything left blank will appear on the site as a clearly marked placeholder until you confirm it.

## 1. Business details (minimum needed to sell online)
1. Is the business a **limited company** or a **sole trader**, and what is its legal name? (If a limited company: the company number and registered office address.)
2. Which postal address can we show on the website? UK law requires one for online sales. A registered office or business address is fine; it does not need to be a home address if another is available.
3. Is the business **VAT registered**? If not, prices will be shown without "incl. VAT" wording, and the price list should be confirmed on that basis.
4. Customer phone number (if any) and opening hours.

## 2. Service area and installation
5. Where do you install: the whole UK, or specific regions, counties or postcodes?
6. Who carries out installations: your own electricians or a partner company?
7. Which accreditations do the installers hold (for example NICEIC, NAPIT, OZEV-approved installer, TrustMark)? Please send certificate numbers. We will only show what you can evidence.
8. What exactly is included in a "standard installation"? For example: maximum cable run (metres), wall drilling, fitting to the consumer unit, earthing or PEN protection, surge protection, DNO notification, certification and handover.
9. What counts as extra work, and the price for each common extra (extra cable per metre, consumer unit upgrade, groundworks or trenching, mounting post fitting, etc.)?
10. Do you do a survey before installing? Is it remote (photos/video) or a home visit, and is it free?
11. Typical time from order to installation.
12. What areas or homes can't you install for (for example on-street parking only, listed buildings, flats without freeholder permission)?

## 3. Products
13. **EVTRON tethered charger:** please confirm the four colours and their names. We have Mythos Black, Space Grey and Midnight Green. What is the red one called?
14. Cable lengths: is 7.5m the standard supplied length? Is 10m actually available, and at what price? (5m will not be offered unless it is in stock.)
15. Does the EVTRON charger connect to the internet (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or 4G) as standard? Which app does it use, and what is the app called? **Important:** home chargers sold in Great Britain must legally be "smart" (connected, with default off-peak charging). Please confirm the charger complies and send the supplier's statement of compliance.
16. Is dynamic load balancing with a CT clamp included with the EVTRON charger as standard?
17. Does the EVTRON charger include PEN fault protection and surge protection, or are these fitted separately during installation?
18. **Fox tethered charger:** please confirm the exact model (we believe it is the Fox ESS L Series L07P) and the four colours: black, blue, purple and pink.
19. **Fox untethered charger:** please confirm the exact model (we believe it is the Fox ESS A Series) in **white**.
20. For the Fox chargers' solar feature: what do customers need for it to work (a Fox inverter, or a Fox smart meter with other inverter brands)?
21. Are your chargers on the government's eligible chargepoint model list for OZEV grants?
22. Please confirm all prices: EVTRON tethered £1,295, Fox tethered £995 and Fox untethered £945, all fully installed (and whether VAT applies; see question 3).
23. Accessories: are the black post (£200) and steel post (£280) supplied **and fitted** at that price? Any other accessories to sell?

## 4. Warranty, returns and payments
24. Warranty: please confirm the 3-year standard warranty, and what the £89.99 extension (to 5 years) covers. Who handles warranty claims, you or the manufacturer?
25. Cancellation and refund policy, including what happens if the survey shows a home isn't suitable after payment.
26. Instalments: do you want Klarna (via Stripe), PayPal Pay in 3, or both? Each needs approval on your account.
27. Do you offer any discounts (for example NHS or Blue Light, referral codes)?
