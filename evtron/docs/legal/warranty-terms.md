## What’s covered

Every EV-Tron charger comes with a 3-year warranty. Upgrade to 5 years in total for extra peace of mind, for only £89.99 when you order.

The warranty covers faults caused by defects in materials or manufacture, under normal domestic use. It starts on the date your charger is installed, or for a charger bought on its own, the date it’s delivered.

## If you bought the charger on its own

Your charger must be installed by a qualified electrician, in line with BS 7671 (the IET Wiring Regulations). If you make a claim, we may ask for a copy of the electrical installation certificate.

## How to make a claim

- Email info@evtron.co.uk with your order number and a short description of the problem.
- Photos or a short video help us diagnose the fault quickly, often remotely.
- Depending on what’s needed, we’ll fix it remotely, arrange a repair, or replace the charger with the same or an equivalent model.

## What isn’t covered

- Accidental damage, misuse, or damage from not following the user manual
- Repairs, modifications or opening of the charger by anyone we haven’t authorised
- Installation not carried out by a qualified electrician or not to BS 7671 (chargers bought on their own)
- Faults caused by the electrical supply, fire, flood or other events outside the charger
- Normal wear and tear, and cosmetic marks that don’t affect how the charger works

## Your legal rights

This warranty is in addition to your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, and doesn’t affect them. Goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. If a fault appears within 6 months, it’s assumed to have been there from the start unless shown otherwise. You can make a claim for up to 6 years in England and Wales, or 5 years in Scotland.
