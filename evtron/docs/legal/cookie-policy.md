## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Some are essential for the site to work. Others help a site understand how it’s used, or show advertising.

## The cookies we use

We currently use only essential cookies. These are needed for the website to work, so they don’t need your consent:

- **Basket and checkout** (WooCommerce): remember what’s in your basket and keep your order session secure, e.g. woocommerce_cart_hash, woocommerce_items_in_cart and wp_woocommerce_session.
- **Accounts** (WordPress): keep you logged in if you have an account, e.g. wordpress_logged_in and wordpress_sec.
- **Payments**: our payment providers, such as Stripe and PayPal, may set cookies during checkout to process your payment securely and prevent fraud.

We don’t currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

## If this changes

If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we’ll update this policy and ask for your consent first, with a clear choice to accept or reject them.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, parts of the site such as the basket and checkout won’t work.
