## Your right to cancel

If you buy from us online or by phone, you can cancel within 14 days without giving a reason. This is your right under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

- **Products we deliver** (a charger on its own, a cable or an accessory): the 14 days start the day after you receive the item.
- **Supply and install orders**: the 14 days start the day after you accept your installation quote.

To cancel, email info@evtron.co.uk with your name, order number and the items you’re cancelling. You don’t need to use a form, but you must tell us before the 14 days end.

## Returning a product

Send the product back within 14 days of telling us you’re cancelling. We’ll give you the returns address.

- Return items unused, with all parts, in their original packaging where possible.
- You can handle a product to check it, as you would in a shop. If it’s been used or handled beyond that, we may reduce your refund to reflect its reduced value.
- Unless the product is faulty or not as described, you pay the cost of sending it back. Pack it carefully, and use a tracked service for valuable items.

## Supply and install orders

If you cancel before any work has started, you get a full refund.

If you asked us to start within your 14-day cancellation period, for example by booking your installation, and then cancel, we may charge for work already carried out up to that point. That covers things like delivering the charger to your installer or a visit that has taken place.

Once your charger has been installed, it can’t be returned because you’ve changed your mind. You’re still fully protected if anything is wrong with it (see below).

## If something is wrong

If a product is faulty, damaged on arrival or not as described, contact us at info@evtron.co.uk as soon as possible, with photos if you can. You’re protected by the Consumer Rights Act 2015:

- **Within 30 days** of delivery or installation, you can reject a faulty product for a full refund.
- **After 30 days**, we’ll repair or replace it. If that isn’t possible or doesn’t fix the problem, you can ask for a price reduction or a refund.

For faults with your charger, see our warranty terms on the Warranty page.

## Offers and free gifts

If an order came with a free gift or free upgrade and you cancel it, the gift must be returned unused with the rest of the order, or we may deduct its value from your refund.

## Refunds

We refund you within 14 days of receiving the returned product, or of being told you’re cancelling if there’s nothing to return. Refunds go back to your original payment method.

If you cancel a whole order, we also refund the standard delivery charge you paid. If you chose a more expensive delivery option, we only refund the standard rate.

## Need help?

Email info@evtron.co.uk or sales@evtron.co.uk, Monday to Saturday, 8:30am to 6:30pm.
