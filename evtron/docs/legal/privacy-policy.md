## What we collect

- **Contact details**: your name, email address, phone number, and your address or postcode.
- **Order details**: what you buy, delivery and installation addresses, and your order history. Payments are handled by our payment providers. We don’t see or store your full card details.
- **Survey information**: details, photos or videos you send us about your home, parking and electrical supply, so we can plan your installation.
- **Messages**: what you tell us through our forms, by email or by phone, including callback requests.
- **Business enquiries**: if you apply to join our installer network or enquire as a dealership, your business details and answers to our questions.
- **Website data**: basic technical information needed to run the site securely, and the cookies described in our Cookie policy.

## How we use it, and why

- **To supply and install your charger** and deliver your order (to perform our contract with you).
- **To answer your enquiries** and call you back when you ask us to (our legitimate interest in responding to you, or to take steps before a contract at your request).
- **To provide warranty, aftercare and support** (contract, and our legal obligations).
- **To keep records** for accounting, tax and electrical safety, such as installation certificates and network operator notifications (legal obligation).
- **To work with installers and dealerships** who apply or enquire (legitimate interests).
- **To keep our website and payments secure** and prevent fraud (legitimate interests).

We don’t send marketing emails unless you’ve agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

## Who we share it with

We share only what’s needed with:

- **Our installers**, to carry out your survey and installation
- **Payment providers** such as Stripe and PayPal, to take payment
- **Delivery companies**, to deliver your order
- **Your electricity network operator (DNO)**, when we notify them of your installation
- **Service providers** who host our website and send our email
- **Professional advisers, and authorities** where the law requires it

We never sell your personal information.

## International transfers

Some of our providers, such as payment providers, may process data outside the UK. When they do, appropriate safeguards are in place, such as UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

## How long we keep it

- **Orders, invoices and installation records**: 6 years after your order, or until your warranty ends if that’s later, to meet legal and warranty obligations.
- **Enquiries that don’t lead to an order**: up to 12 months.
- **Installer and dealership applications** we don’t take forward: up to 12 months.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of your personal information
- ask us to correct or delete it
- ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to how we use it
- ask us to transfer it to you or another organisation
- withdraw your consent, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, email info@evtron.co.uk. We’ll reply within one month.

## Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator.
