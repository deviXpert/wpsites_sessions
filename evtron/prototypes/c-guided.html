Questions people ask first

Will it work with my car?

All three chargers use Type 2, which most battery-electric and plug-in hybrid cars sold in the UK use. Check your car's charging port if you're unsure.

Tethered or untethered: what's the difference?

A tethered charger has the cable attached, so you just plug in. An untethered charger has a socket, and you use your own Type 2 cable.

Can I charge from my solar panels?

The two Fox chargers can use surplus solar power. What you need depends on your inverter [requirements TBC with Fox].

Am I eligible for a government grant?

The OZEV grant (up to £500) is for renters and flat owners with private off-street parking, until 31 March 2027. People who own a house aren't eligible. Source: gov.uk.
