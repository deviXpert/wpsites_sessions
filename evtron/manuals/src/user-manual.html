01 · Safety
01 · Safety first

Please read before use.

Your charger works with mains electricity. These rules protect you, your home and your vehicle.

Using the charger

  • Never immerse the connector or the control box in water. Protect the connector from rain, heat and moisture.
  • Don't use the charger if the unit, the cable or the connector is damaged.
  • Don't touch conductors while charging, and never put your fingers into the connector opening.
  • Don't leave the charging cable coiled while you charge. Uncoil it fully.
  • Never use extension leads or adapters.

Electrical safety

  • The charger must be connected to a properly earthed circuit.
  • Only a qualified electrician may install, open or service the unit. There is a risk of high-voltage electric shock inside.
  • For 7 kW home charging, copper supply cable of 6 mm² or larger is recommended. See page 07 for minimum sizes.
If something seems wrong

If the red light flashes, the cable is damaged or the unit is hot, smells or makes noise: press the emergency stop on the side of the unit, stop using it and contact us.

05 · Installation
05 · Installation · for electricians

Mounting and wiring.

Qualified electricians only

Install in line with BS 7671 and the IET Code of Practice for EV charging equipment. Assess the earthing arrangement and provide PEN fault protection where required. Never work live.

Wall mounting

1

Choose a load-bearing wall. Leave clearance for cooling, holster access and cable routing.

2

Fix the wall bracket first, then secure the control-box bracket and the holster.

3

Wire the input to the applicable regulations and make sure protective earth is connected.

Input lead 1000 ± 20 mm · charging cable 5000 ± 50 mm

Minimum supply cable size

Charging current8 A16 A24 A32 A
Copper cross-section1.5 mm²2.5 mm²4 mm²6 mm²

Don't reuse old cables. Undersized cable can overheat. Faults caused by cable outside these specifications aren't covered.

06 · How to charge
06 · How to charge

Four steps to a full battery.

01

Check the E-stop

Make sure the emergency stop is released. If it's pressed in, turn it clockwise to reset.

02

Connect

Take the connector from its holster and push it fully into your vehicle's Type 2 inlet.

03

Start

Charging starts automatically in plug-and-charge mode. Otherwise tap your RFID card or start it in the app.

04

Finish

Stop charging first, then unplug. Hang the connector back in its holster.

Auto stop

Charging stops by itself when your vehicle is full. In RFID or app mode, the charger only supplies power once you've authorised the session.

Why the car shows less than 7 kW

Your vehicle may report slightly less power than the charger supplies. This is normal: some energy is used in conversion.
