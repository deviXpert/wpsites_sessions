01 · Overview

About your charger

For battery-electric, plug-in hybrid and range-extender vehicles with a Type 2 (European 7-pin) AC charging inlet. Suitable for home, commercial and fixed installations.

  • Start charging by plugging in, with an RFID card, or from the app, including over Bluetooth.
  • App control and scheduling, with automatic shut-off when charging is complete.
  • Live readings: power, voltage, current, temperature and charging time.
  • Protection against over-temperature, over-voltage, prolonged under-voltage, over-current and leakage current.

Get set up in minutes.

Your charger uses the WeGoodCharger app. Your installer will help you set it up on the day.

iPhone

Scan, or search “WeGoodCharger” in the App Store.

Android

Scan for the latest download link at evtron.co.uk/app

1Choose your language

2Adjust your settings

Tap Setting to open the parameter screen. Set what you need, confirm, then exit.

06 · Safety and support
06 · Protection functions

Built-in protection.

ProtectionWhat happens
Over-voltageCharging stops when the voltage exceeds 270 V.
Prolonged under-voltageCharging stops when the voltage stays below 90 V.
Over-currentCharging stops when the current exceeds 1.1 × the rated current.
Leakage currentIf leakage between the vehicle and the control box exceeds 30 mA, power is cut and charging stops.
Over-temperatureThe charging current is reduced, then restored as the unit cools. Charge in a cool, well-ventilated place.
If it stops responding

If the charger won't connect, stops responding or shows a fault, switch it off for 3 seconds and back on. If the fault clears, carry on as normal. If not, contact us.

07 · Precautions
  • Don't reuse old cables. Minimum copper size: 1.5 mm² at 8 A, 2.5 mm² at 16 A, 4 mm² at 24 A, 6 mm² at 32 A. Undersized cable can overheat.
  • The charger must be properly earthed.
  • Never put your fingers into the connector opening.
  • Don't use the charger if it or its cable is damaged.
  • Only qualified persons may open or repair the unit. There is a risk of high-voltage electric shock inside.
