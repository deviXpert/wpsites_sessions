What Is Wheel-Lift Recovery?

A wheel-lift recovery system is designed to lift one end of a vehicle off the ground so it can be towed by a recovery vehicle.

Unlike a flatbed truck, where the complete vehicle is loaded onto a platform, wheel-lift recovery supports and lifts the vehicle by its wheels. The appropriate recovery method depends on the vehicle, its condition, location and the distance it needs to be transported.

This makes wheel-lift recovery a useful option for certain vehicles that need towing after a breakdown or when they cannot be driven.
